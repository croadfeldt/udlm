A tenant boundary is an **operational** boundary, not an audit or visibility one — tenancy governs may-I-read/act/own (exclusive, structural, `CTX-001`); what is *visible* and what is *recorded* are separate surfaces with their own governance | Accepted (upstream) — requires engineering ratification

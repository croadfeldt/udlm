Findings — one shape for every detected condition (drift, tamper, staleness, cadence-miss): a **sealed ledger interpretation** with open-once/close-by-citing-resolution lifecycle, never a record field; drift = divergence outside an active convergence (ADR-052 consulted first), detected event-driven, severity + field relevance by policy; current status always derived on read; the accept mechanism deliberately open (delegated to the control plane OBL-003). Consumes ADR-059; ratifies separately | Proposed

**A sealed record keeps the algorithm spelling it was sealed under** (amends row 120). Row 120 respelled the integrity algorithm `sha256-jcs` → `sha256_jcs`, and `verify()` then refused every record sealed before it: every chain in every estate failed INT-001/002, and a sealed record cannot be re-sealed without rewriting its history (ADR-051). Both spellings name one construction (SHA-256 over the RFC 8785 form of the record minus `integrity`), and the name is not part of what is hashed, so `integrity_chain.ALGORITHMS` accepts both, the schema enum lists both, and `seal()` writes only `sha256_jcs`. A genuinely different construction is a new entry with its own rule in `head_for`, never a respelling. Self-test: a pre-row-120 record and a mixed chain (old-spelling root, new-spelling head) verify; an unknown name is refused. Raised by the estate session.

Completes ADR-054's other half — `reference_data` did double duty (orthogonal **context**, moved to edges by ADR-054, **and** curated **vocabulary** binding). Ruling: a curated vocabulary is **not a new construct** — it rides on the existing **`SharedDataElement`** (ADR-038: *scope position IS portability*) and the **vocabulary-intake ladder** (ADR-039: `proposed→canonical` + profile-gated strictness = standardization). Retirement relocates term storage from `reference_data` layers into the scoped-element model, so portability + standardization become structural. A first draft proposing a new `Codelist` entity was **rejected** (parallel construct). `reference_data` retired entirely | Proposed

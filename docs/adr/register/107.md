**The layer structure is the layer schema.** Layering-and-versioning §4.3 showed seven fields `layer.schema.json` does not have and none of the nine it does. It is now a table of the schema's fields in six groups (identity, ownership, position, targeting, data, bounds), plus an example marked `# schema: layer.schema.json` that validates. A second table says what replaced each older field. `priority` is `precedence_class` and `precedence_order`. `concern_tags`, `compatibility` and `activation_condition` are `covers` and `applies_on`. Per-field override markers are `limits` and `narrow_only`. `usage` is `description`. `scm_location` is version control. Override control (§5a) now opens with what has a schema surface (`limits`, provenance source `override`, the `override` policy type) and what does not; its examples are marked illustrative and the missing fields are listed on #632. The annex's outdated complete-layer example points at §4.3 and the shipped example layers. Closes #635.

**Service and process fields earn their place** (row 108 applied to Workload, LogStore, LogShipper, PowerFeed and Automation.OSPatch, #664). Workload 0.11.0 moves `endpoints` from the spec to the outputs: how a service is reached is what the provider reports, and Template.Application already said so. Health is `status.conditions`, not an output (data-model-core §3): Workload drops `ready`, LogStore 0.3.0 and LogShipper 0.10.0 drop `status`. Fields restated through an edge go: LogShipper `shipping_to` echoed the store's `intake_endpoints` its `sink` edge binds; PowerFeed 0.9.0 drops `status`, `battery_charge` and `runtime_seconds`, the UPS's outputs read through `supplied_by`. Automation.OSPatch 1.4.0 drops `last_run_status`, `last_run_timestamp` and `packages_updated`: each run is a Job whose `results` and `completed_at` carry them. PowerFeed and OSPatch now publish no outputs of their own and say so. Maintainer rulings 2026-10-25. Row 115 is in flight.

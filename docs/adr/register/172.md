**The versioning rules of row 171 are CI gates** (REG-011, REG-014, REG-017, REG-018; registry-governance.md, VERSIONING.md). Row 171 said how versions carry down the class tree and what a provider's bound version promises. Four checks now fail a change that breaks those rules. A descendant left behind its ancestor is refused (REG-017, new). A served spec whose change outgrows its bump, or whose version goes backwards, is refused (REG-011). A provider binding to a version the registry never published is refused (REG-014, for provider bindings; must-reject 043). A provider registration that changes a binding without a sufficient bump of its own is refused (REG-018, new). The checks fail a change; they do not yet write the refusal record REG-015 describes. Process-family bindings still carry no version, and placement is not checked here. Maintainer ruling 2026-11-18.

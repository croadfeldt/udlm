**The Network folder dissolves** (ADR-082 applied). `IPAddress`, `IPAddressPool`, `Subnet`, `VLAN`, `VirtualNetwork`, `NetworkGateway`, `NetworkSwitch`, `AddressService`, `DHCPScope`, `DNSZone` and `ConnectionProfile` are Bases by the Base test, filed under `network` (a switch also under `hardware`, an address service also under `services`). `zone` and `tier` have one home in `common-elements.schema.json` and every network Base declares them by `$ref`; the `network_tier` vocabulary term is canonical at the `Resource` family. `VLAN` is a Base, not a Type of `VirtualNetwork`; DHCP/DNS and host/overlay dialects become Types when they carry elements. Same uuids; map in `registry/renames.yaml`. Maintainer ruling 2026-09-29.

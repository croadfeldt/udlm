**Each capability has a profile in the registration** (WP3 slice 2, #634). `capability_profiles` holds one profile per verb: realize_resources, serve_data, composite_service, authenticate, federate and execute_workflows. A profile needs its verb among the declared capabilities; `validate.py` checks it. The resource advertisement, the matched-capabilities response and the capability registry entry get their own definitions, since a provider does not submit them. Every provider-contract §8, §9 and §10.2 example is schema-checked, as are the cancellation and discovery blocks in operational-models. Dropped: `cost_metadata` (ADR-COST-001), `constituent_provider_types` and `resource_types_composed` (retired types; the class carries constituents), product names, `dcm_version`, and the registry entry's separate verb and domain. Experimental.

**A replica says what it copies.** Volume gains `replica_of`, a Reference to the Volume it continuously copies (a ZFS send/receive target, a Ceph RBD mirror, an array's async replica), with a `replica_of` edge declared on the replica. Unlike `data_source` (row 122, filled once), a replica keeps following its source. The fact is data: restore and disaster-recovery planning read where a volume's copies are, replica-lag monitoring derives its targets from the pairs, and drift checks know a replica is written only by replication. Schedule and retention stay policy, held by the control plane (Volume.ZFS says so already). No standard is adopted: Kubernetes has none for replication (csi-addons VolumeReplication is not a Kubernetes API).

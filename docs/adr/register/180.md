**A volume reports whether it holds a filesystem, so a policy can tell an empty volume from one with data** (maintainer ruling 2026-11-23). Machine 2.8.0 can mount a local Volume (row 168), and review asked who prepares it and how its data is protected. That is policy, not the model. A policy that never formats a volume with data needs to know what is on it now, and `data_source` says only how the volume started. `Volume` 0.20.0 adds the output `filesystem`: whether the provider found one when it last looked (`found`) and which (`fs_type`). The provider fills it in. Absent means it has not reported, never that the volume is empty. Every existing record keeps its meaning.

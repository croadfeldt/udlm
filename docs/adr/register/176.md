**An access point and a UPS run firmware, so a bill of materials can describe one and the `affected` walk reaches it.** Row 173's walk yields a device as affected when a bill of materials describes it, and listed switches, gateways and management controllers. Two classes that also state the firmware they run were missing: `WirelessAccessPoint` (row 164) and `UPS`. An advisory about their firmware therefore reached nothing. `SoftwareBillOfMaterials` takes a MINOR to 0.2.0 for two more `describes` targets, and the walk table moves to 0.2.0 with both in its `described_assets` step. Nothing else about the walk changes. A consumer that reads the table follows the new targets with no change of its own (DEP-019). `Printer` (row 175) joins the same way once its class is in.

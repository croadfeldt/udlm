**A natural key that is unique only within a scope carries the scope.** Discovered edges name a target by a `correlation_ids` scheme and value, and no scheme named a VLAN or a subnet. The estate keyed tags as `provider-id: vlan:<id>`, which is wrong on two fabrics and wrong where duplicates are deliberate, such as an isolated test copy. Three schemes join the list. `segment-id` is `<observer>/<domain>/<id>`, rooted at the isolation domain the probe observed it in. `subnet-prefix` is `<segment>#<cidr>`. `gateway-mac` is a second key that tells a masqueraded copy from the original. A record may carry several keys. Resolution matches on what it has and refuses a tie as ambiguous, never merging on a guess. Four-states §2.4 states the rule. Maintainer ruling 2026-10-26. Closes #675.

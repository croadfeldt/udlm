**Every requested record names its root request.** `root_request_uuid` (`DEP-015`) is the record_uuid of the requested record at the root of the order. A root names itself; a record the control plane expanded from a declared dependency or a composition names the root's. This is causation; `parent_composite_uuid` (CMP-007) is structure. Required on requested records, refused on the other kinds. Maintainer ruling 2026-09-28, relayed.

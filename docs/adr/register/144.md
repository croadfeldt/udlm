**Nothing is stable until it is used and validated.** There are no completed specs yet. A part becomes stable once a running implementation has used it and the conformance suite has judged its records; until then it is experimental, whatever it looks like on paper. Every part in `registry/stability.yaml` is experimental today, and the reasons that made 89 of them candidates are kept on their rows. The stable list is earned one part at a time, each by a register row. The 1.0 tag covers the stable parts at that time, and the maintainer decides when that set is enough. The enum-casing gate no longer leans on a stable set: it fails everywhere, with the ledger action list the one named exemption. Maintainer ruling 2026-11-03.

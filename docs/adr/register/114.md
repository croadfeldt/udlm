**Storage fields earn their place** (row 108 applied to StoragePool and Volume.ZFS, #664). StoragePool 0.7.0 drops the `health` element, an observed fact in the spec whose own description deferred to the `redundancy_status` output, and the `degraded` output, which was `redundancy_status != healthy`; health is `status.conditions`, not a field (data-model-core §3). Its `capacity` keeps `size` and drops `allocated` and `free`: observed, and `free` is `size - allocated`; `usable_capacity` is the realized fact. Volume.ZFS 0.8.0 drops `used`, space consumed as the provider reports it, which nothing read. Maintainer rulings 2026-10-25.

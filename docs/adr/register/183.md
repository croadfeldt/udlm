**The conformance suite checks a class's structural relationships on records.** Row 170 made `describes` on `SoftwareBillOfMaterials` structural with cardinality `1..1`, and nothing checked a record against it. Suite 1.1.0 adds `REL-002`: a requested, realized or discovered head must carry, for every relationship its class marks structural, as many edges as the cardinality allows. Templates that share a name are one relation and are counted together. An edge that names no relation, as on a discovered record, is counted by its target's class, read from the records beside it. A target that is not among them makes the count unjudged, which is reported and does not fail. Must-reject cases 044 and 045 are a bill of materials with no subject and one with two. A record that suite 1.0.0 passed can fail 1.1.0. Asked for in review of row 170.

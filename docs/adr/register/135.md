**A provider registration has a schema** (WP3 slice 1, #634). `registry/provider-registration.schema.json` is what a provider submits: identity, declared capabilities, the sovereignty claim, accreditation references, accepted roles, requested data, endpoints, certificate, reachability and the telemetry descriptor (§7). It has no field for trust posture, admitted capabilities or lifecycle state; those are the control plane's, in the registration verdict. `validate.py` checks that each declared capability is a provider-capability term, and that a verdict dispositions only capabilities its provider declared. The worked registration is the provider the verdict example decides. Contract §2 and §7 examples are now schema-checked. `status`, `provisioned_by` and `certifications` are dropped. Experimental. Slice 2 covers the §8 profiles and §10.2.

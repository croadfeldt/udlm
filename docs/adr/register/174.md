**Whether a vulnerability is being exploited is derived, not stored** (policy fact `vulnerability.exploitation`; knowledge-family.md §4.6, #TBD). Severity says how bad a vulnerability could be and not whether anyone is using it. A party other than the advisory says that, a known-exploited catalog or a vendor, on its own clock, so a copy on the `Vulnerability` record would go stale and hide who said it (DRV-001). It is a policy fact, computed when a decision is evaluated, with the values of the SSVC Exploitation decision point: none, poc, active. A catalog entry is evidence for `active`. No report means the fact is absent, never `none`. The decision's audit snapshot keeps the value used. `Vulnerability` is unchanged. SSVC's Automatable is not adopted until a source the estate reads states it, and an exploit-prediction score is not carried. Proposed 2026-11-18 as two class elements, reworked 2026-11-19 on review, not yet ruled.

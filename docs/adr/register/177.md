**Firmware is its own record, and a device is matched to it the way a container is matched to its image** (maintainer ruling 2026-11-19). A device stated the firmware version it runs, and nothing could say what an advisory about that firmware reached. `FirmwareImage` 0.1.0 is one build: maker, model and version, as a device states them. A bill of materials describes the build, so every device on it shares one document (`SoftwareBillOfMaterials` 0.2.0). No edge ties a device to its build. The `affected` walk matches them when the answer is read (DRV-001), from the maker, model and firmware version the device already states. One value does not name a build, so a join may now compare several (walks.yaml 0.2.0). The first devices are `NetworkSwitch`, `NetworkGateway` and `WirelessAccessPoint`. Key scheme: `firmware_build`.

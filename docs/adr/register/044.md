**Consumer conformance surface.** A consumer declares its read surface in `registry/consumers/*.yaml`: the types it reads and the version it last verified against, or `consumes_all_types` for an envelope-level reader. The registry gates on that declaration. Every declared type must exist. No declared version may be ahead of the registry. Every registry type must be consumed or explicitly acknowledged in `unconsumed.yaml`, which is regenerated and compared, ratchet style. Promotion from `declared` to `verified` is the consumer's own CI act. | Ruled — row-only; content home: `registry/consumers/` + `tests/check_consumer_conformance.py`

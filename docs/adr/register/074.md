**Adopt SLSA and in-toto; adopt CTRF and JUnit XML for test results.** SLSA and the in-toto Attestation Framework move from PATTERN to CANONICAL in the standards register. An in-toto Statement is the signed projection of a UDLM record; its subject is the record's `integrity.head`, and the join runs from statement to record. SLSA levels are the producer's to earn; UDLM carries them. Field-level provenance stays UDLM's own, since SLSA has no equivalent. `TestEvidence` is the first producer (#591). CTRF is the preferred test-report format and JUnit XML is accepted, both by reference.

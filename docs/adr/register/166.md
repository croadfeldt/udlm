**A workload states its exposure; the provider reports its endpoints** (Workload 0.12.0, #TBD). Row 116 moved `endpoints` to the outputs because how a service is reached is what the provider reports. That left the ask with no home: that a service is meant to be reachable at a name, from the estate or from the internet, is a decision, and a security fact. The new optional `exposure` element carries it (`names`, `visibility` on the Container `network.ports` codelist loopback/internal/external, `protocols`). It is stated, never derived from where the service runs. The `endpoints` output gets an item schema: `name`, `url` or `host` and `port`, `protocol`, `via` (route, ingress, load_balancer, node_port, published_port, listener, port_forward) and `visibility`, which only a collector at the boundary sets. A gate can now compare the ask with the report and flag a service reachable further than it asked to be. TOSCA's Endpoint capability grounds both. Maintainer ruling 2026-11-09.

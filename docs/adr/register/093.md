**A log shipper binds a log store, in the terms the syslog and OpenTelemetry standards already use.** `LogShipper` 0.8.0 (breaking): its source is a `contained_by` edge to a Machine, BMC, switch, gateway or cluster, and its destination a `binds_to` edge to the new `LogStore` Base (filed under `data`), borrowing the store's published intake. The Type tier is the wire protocol: `LogShipper.Syslog` (RFC 9742 transport and facility filter) and `LogShipper.OTLP`. Severity uses RFC 5424 names; labels are OpenTelemetry resource attributes. No Provider Classes. Maintainer ruling 2026-10-10.

**`artifact_metadata` is retired; each of its jobs has a home.** The block appeared in 23 places across 15 spec documents and in no schema. Identity, version and status are the record's own top-level fields. Who did what is the audit record of each submission. What changed is version control. Who may author a record is its `contributor` block. Display data is `metadata`. Layering-and-versioning §4b is now that mapping. A record in the store is `active`, `deprecated` or `retired`, the three states the schemas allow. `developing` and `proposed` are stages of a change before it merges; policy shadow execution happens during review. An accountable owner and their contact have no schema field yet: §4b.4 states the notifications as a control-plane obligation, and #632 lists the fields. Every example that carried the block now carries the record's own fields, marked illustrative. Part of #635; the layer structure in §4.3 follows separately.

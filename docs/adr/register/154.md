**Security, tenancy and sovereignty are explicit; entity-relationships has a schema for every field it shows** (#632; Chris, 2026-11-04). A cross-tenant edge now states `target_tenant_uuid` and names its grant in `authorization_ref` (CTX-010, new). The grant gate verifies them and no longer searches for a grant that fits. **Behaviour change:** an edge that names no grant is refused even when one exists. The worked example gains a successor record that states both; must-reject 033 and 034 cover the new refusals. The grant gate's rule ID moves from GRD-001, which the graduation ladder owns, to CTX-010. In entity-relationships: allocations use the resource advertisement and grant-naming edges; sharing is the class's `ownership_model`; lifecycle actions are policy; external lookups and bundle expansion are the implementation's. Undefined fields are gone.

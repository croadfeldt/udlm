**A volume sits in its pool, and a pool in its cluster: containment, not a dependency.** uc-06 named a pool as a volume's capacity source; `Volume` declared only `provisioned_by` a StorageCluster, and `Volume.ZFS` named its pool as a `depends_on`. Ruling 2026-11-04: use relationship mechanics for dependency and encapsulation. `Volume` (0.19.0) declares `contained_by` a StoragePool, relation `pool`, optional at the base since a cloud block volume shows no pool; `Volume.ZFS` (0.9.0) tightens it to exactly one, as containment. `StoragePool` (0.8.0) declares `contained_by` a StorageCluster when clustered, beside its host edge. A volume's cluster is then derived, the nearest StorageCluster up its containment chain, by the same rule that derives place (row 159); `provisioned_by` stays for a volume with no pool, and naming both in disagreement is a finding. The estate's ZFS datasets re-pin with one edge-type change, in the 1.0 re-pin.

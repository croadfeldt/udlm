**A host, a switch and a power feed say where they are.** Only UPS declared a `located_in` edge to a Location, while Location's own notes named Machine.BareMetalHost as the equipment that declares its location. The model contradicted itself. An estate that had modeled a remote lab as a second tenant had nowhere to put the place once tenancy was corrected to ownership. `Machine.BareMetalHost` (0.18.0), `NetworkSwitch` (0.12.0) and `PowerFeed` (0.10.0) now carry the same optional edge UPS has: `contained_by` a Location, named `located_in`. Placement is stated on the record and never inferred from power or network; a host is not at a site because it draws from that site's UPS. Residency reads this edge. VMs and containers follow by containment and need none. Estate ask 2026-11-04, under the maintainer's rule that security, tenancy and sovereignty are explicit and never inferred (row 154).

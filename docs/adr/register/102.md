**Identity and address family are defined once.** `common-elements.schema.json` gains `identity`: manufacturer, model, serial number, asset tag, location and role, open so a class adds its own discriminators beside it. The eight classes that declared their own identity block — BareMetalHost, GPU, NetworkInterface, Processor, StorageDevice, NetworkSwitch, VirtualNetwork, UPS — `$ref` it and keep their class-specific descriptions and extra keys (`wwn`, `mac_address`, `sku`, `chassis_id`, `system_name`), a REVISION each. `ip_family` drops `dual`, because an address, pool, subnet or DHCP scope has exactly one family; the new `ip_families` keeps `dual` for a gateway that routes both. Every address-family element `$ref`s one of the two: IPAddress 0.12.0, IPAddressPool 0.9.0 and Subnet 0.3.0 narrow (MINOR); DHCPScope and NetworkGateway accept what they did (REVISION). Closes #644.

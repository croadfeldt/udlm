**The Discovered record is the observed state, and a value's place says what it is** (maintainer ruling 2026-11-23; four-states.md, data-model-core.md). Observed and discovered are one thing: what exists now, as a provider sees it. That is the role of Kubernetes `status`, and only a provider writes it. A discovery source is a provider: an Information Provider, declaring `serve_data` (provider-contract §8). Whether an observation counts, and who owns what it saw, is policy (row 182). Intent and Requested hold what should be, Realized what the provider built, Discovered what is there now. A class's part says what can be asked for: an element is a value a requester can set, an output a value only a provider supplies. Both appear in a Discovered record as observed. A value keeps one name everywhere; no `observed_` or `current_` prefix. Knowledge classes, with no desired state, are exempt.

The absolute provider pin — **UDLM fixes the invariant** (the `effective_capabilities` ceiling always applies at the dispatch boundary; a pin is preference *among eligible* providers, never a bypass; an ineligible pin → `placement.capability_mismatch` before dispatch); **whether a deliberate bypass exists is an operational policy — a control plane obligation**, priced through the existing override-record flow (`policy-contract.md` §18). Also settles the corpus typing: capability mismatch is `policy_violation`, not `provider.*`. Settled by the ADR-008 peer test | Proposed

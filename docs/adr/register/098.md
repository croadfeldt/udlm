**One uuid definition per version.** `common-elements.schema.json` gains `uuid_v4` and `uuid_v7`, and all 99 uuid fields in the record schemas, facets and classes `$ref` one of them instead of declaring a pattern inline. The version follows identifier-scheme §2.1: v4 for an entity, definition, tenant, actor, provider or request; v7 for a time-ordered artifact: a state record, audit-chain leaf, audit record, commit-log entry, audit session or event. Of the 30 fields that accepted v4 or v7, 22 now take v4 and 8 take v7; the five audit-record and commit-log ids are v7 by maintainer ruling 2026-10-20, and §2.1's table now names them. The 5 bare `format: uuid` fields, which checked nothing, and the class schema's own `uuid`, which took any hex string, now take v4. Grouping.Tenant and Grouping.Authorization take a REVISION bump: their fields were already v4. A new gate, `tests/check_uuid_refs.py`, refuses an inline uuid pattern. Closes #639.

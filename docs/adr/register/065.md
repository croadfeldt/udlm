UDLM specifies the policy-engine **contract**, never the predicate language — the engine is a *Provider of decisions* (§7.2a). UDLM owns the fact vocabulary a policy may read, the evaluation context, the decision/constraint shapes, and the audit obligations; the comparison lives in `match.rule`, opaque and unparsed. The removed `operator` enum had forked into five disagreeing spellings, and two of its surfaces (`all_of`/`any_of`, `condition_logic`) were fictional | Proposed

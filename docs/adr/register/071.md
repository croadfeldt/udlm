**One record per lifecycle state.** `registry/state-record.schema.json` defines one closed record shape with four kinds, dispatched on `record_type`: an envelope plus one body per state, one author each. The envelope carries `entity_uuid` (the identity edges point at), `record_uuid` (this record; RFC 9562 v7, time-ordered), `state`, `generation`, `supersedes`, the tenant and class pin, `at`/`time_source` and `integrity`. A requested record names its intent (`intent_ref`). A realized record names its requested (`requested_ref`) and its provider instance as a reference, not a string. Drift compares the latest realized and discovered records, never stored. `realized-entity.schema.json` becomes the READ MODEL assembled from the records, never written (rename to follow). The four kinds are immutable; a change is a new record naming its predecessor (ADR-051).

**A part graduates by a ladder** (`docs/spec/governance/graduation.md`, GRD-001 to GRD-006). Three stages: experimental, candidate, stable. A part becomes a candidate with a worked record, a must-reject case for every rule it carries, readable text, no open blocker, and a register row; that row's date starts the clock. It becomes stable after 90 days as a candidate with no breaking change, once a conformance report from a running implementation has judged at least ten of its records, by a register row that cites the report. A breaking change moves it down a step. One implementation suffices today; two independent ones become a further step when a second exists. Chosen after a review of ten ladders and the CNCF's own (`docs/research/udlm-graduation-research.md`): the Gateway API shape, few stages with numeric criteria. Maintainer ruling 2026-11-03.

**An observer-rooted key is an observation, never a VLAN's identity** (amends row 118). On the estate, one fabric VLAN got a different `segment-id` from each host trunking it. Rooting the key at the switch does not help: a VLAN trunked across three switches is three keys. Inferring one broadcast domain from LLDP and trunk lists is the fabric controller's knowledge, wrong under tag translation, QinQ or a deliberate duplicate. A VLAN record's identity is its owning provider's own identifier, `provider-id`. Observer keys, host- or switch-rooted, are observations that resolve to the record that lists them. UDLM never infers that two observer keys name one VLAN. A fabric with no provider reporting its VLANs has no authoritative VLAN identity; its observed edges stay key-only, a valid state (REF-003). A host-owned bridge or overlay is keyed at the host, which is its provider. Maintainer ruling 2026-10-26, relayed through the estate session.

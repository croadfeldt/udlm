**A Base Class is earned by a shared portability contract.** Its members are things one consumer intent can move between. A grouping whose members share no portability contract is a folder, not a Base. Applied: `Compute` splits three ways. `Container` becomes its own Base, because "run this image" is its own contract; it had inherited `guest_os`, which no container provider can honor. `Compute.Cluster` becomes the `KubernetesCluster` Base, because `pod_cidr` and `service_cidr` are Kubernetes vocabulary, so the class is named for what it is. `Compute` renames to `Machine`, the Base of VM and BareMetalHost, the two that narrow its elements.

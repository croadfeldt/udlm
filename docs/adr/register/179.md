**What a source says an advisory applies to is kept on the advisory, and a package is linked to it when read** (maintainer ruling 2026-11-20). `SoftwarePackage` 0.6.0 retires the `affected_by` edge: it could not say who drew the link or how. `Vulnerability` 0.4.0 replaces `affected_ranges` with `applicability`, one entry per source and configuration in the source's own words. Products are named by purl, CPE or name, versions are VERS, and the hardware an NVD configuration requires is kept. The walk's new `identifier_join` links a package whose purl or CPE names the same product, never by name. Whether its version is in range is the policy fact `vulnerability.version_in_range`, computed when read. A link another party drew is a `VexStatement` 0.2.0 naming that party. UDLM holds the data; a policy decides what it means.

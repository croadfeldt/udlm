**Every field earns its place.** A field needs a genuine use case and an operational benefit: something reads it and acts on it. It is not already carried elsewhere (the four states, provenance, the audit record, version control, a reference) and is not derivable instead of stored. A consumer's choice is a spec element; a derived fact is computed or a condition; an entity's lifecycle is never a second field. Stated in core tenet T7, the tenet about surface. Maintainer directive 2026-10-22; the field-justification sweep (#664) applies it to every class and record schema.

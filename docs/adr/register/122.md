**A volume says where its data came from and where it is reachable** (#680, storage claims slice 1). Volume 0.15.0 gains `data_source`, the Kubernetes PVC `dataSource`: a Reference to a VolumeSnapshot (restore) or another Volume (clone), with `restored_from` / `cloned_from` edges. It is read once at provisioning and kept as lineage, so copied data names its origin. Output `accessible_from` holds References to the narrowest scope that reaches it (host, StorageCluster, Location): the CSI accessible topology. Placement puts a consumer by its volume with it; a move tells a re-bind from a copy. Absent means unknown, never "anywhere". New Base VolumeSnapshot 0.1.0 (Kubernetes VolumeSnapshot / CSI CreateSnapshot) carries `source` and `deletion_policy`, outputs `snapshot_handle` and `restore_size`, all Kubernetes names in snake_case; readiness is a `Ready` status condition. `deletion_policy` is Kubernetes `deletionPolicy`, Delete \| Retain, verbatim and `x-standard` like StorageClass `reclaim_policy`. Continuous replication is not a snapshot and waits for its own row.

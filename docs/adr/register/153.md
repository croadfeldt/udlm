**The audit record and the event envelope name no implementation** (#723 item 8; #503). `dcm_version` becomes `implementation_version` on the audit record and the event envelope, and the envelope's `dcm_instance_uuid` becomes `implementation_instance_uuid`. EVT-001 lists the new names. The normative tier is implementation-neutral, and the conformance declaration already calls the control plane `implementation`. The fields mean what they meant; only their names change. Both parts are experimental, so the rename needs no deprecation.

**ADR-038 amended, four ways** (the record is immutable, ADR-REAL-004). (1) Naming depth is three, not unbounded (CLS-001); `registry/class.schema.json` caps `resource_type` at three segments. Version, capability advertisement and authority remain the cheaper axes. (2) Base, Type and Provider Class are defined normatively in `docs/spec/foundations/class-tiers.md` (`CLS-*`); ADR-038 holds the why, cited not restated. (3) An offering needing consumer-supplied data the Type lacks declares a Provider Class carrying it as elements (CLS-005). ADR-038 §10 distinguished two deployments of one dialect only by advertised capability; ROSA versus ARO showed that is not enough. (4) "All Classes are instantiable" is qualified: ADR-038 §4 said yes for Base, but a category whose members share no abstraction is a folder, `instantiable: false` (CLS-003, ADR-069): `Storage`, `Data`, `Network`, `Security`, `Observability`, `Software`, `Facility`, `Hardware`.

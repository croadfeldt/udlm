# Maintainers

The people who decide what goes into UDLM. A decision is recorded as a row in the decisions
register, `docs/adr/README.md`, dated, newest on top.

| Name | GitHub ID | Company | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chris Roadfeldt | croadfeldt | Independent | Maintainer; rules on every register row |

## How a maintainer is added or removed

A maintainer is added by a register row proposed by an existing maintainer, after at least three
months of reviewed contributions. A maintainer who has not reviewed or ruled for six months is
asked whether they wish to stay; silence for a further month moves them to emeritus, by a register
row. Affiliations are kept current here within 30 days of a change.

## Where decisions are made

In the open: issues and pull requests on this repository, and the register. There is no private
channel for decisions.
